This was part of a security awareness training exercise. Don’t worry — this is a safe environment to practice identifying phishing attempts.

Here are a few signs that the email may have been unsafe:

  • Unexpected urgency — “verify immediately” or “your account will be closed.”
  • Unfamiliar sender or slightly misspelled email address.
  • Links that don’t match the real company website.
  • Attachments you weren’t expecting.
  • Generic greetings like “Dear user” instead of your name.

Practicing these skills helps protect both you and the organization. You’re doing the right thing by learning from this exercise.
